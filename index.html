About

Connecting geospatial science and ecology

I am an ecologist and spatial analyst at the U.S. Geological Survey. I earned a Master of Science in Environmental Sciences and Management, with an emphasis in geospatial studies, from Cal Poly Humboldt.

I have extensive training in geospatial analysis and geographic applications, with an emphasis on interdisciplinary research and software programming. My current work focuses on land-cover and land-use change, conservation biology, urban ecology, and ecosystem services.

I have nine years of professional experience working with geospatial and environmental data. I published my first lead-author study in 2017 while working on sylvatic plague management in Colorado prairie-steppe ecosystems. Since then, I have contributed to numerous scientific publications and developed geospatial products for a range of clients and collaborators.

Interests

I am passionate about natural resource management, conservation, and ecological restoration. My work integrates spatial analysis, environmental modeling, programming, and natural resource management to support keystone species and landscape-scale ecological strategies.

I have also contributed to vaccine research and data-driven decision-making in environmental science. Outside the laboratory and field, I am a trained wildland firefighter and enjoy glassblowing, hiking, camping, hunting, and fishing.
