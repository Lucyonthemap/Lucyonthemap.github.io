01 / About

Combining ecological expertise with computational problem-solving.

I am an ecologist and spatial analyst at the U.S. Geological Survey. I earned a Master of Science in Environmental Sciences and Management, with an emphasis in geospatial studies, from Cal Poly Humboldt.

My work combines geospatial analysis, environmental modeling, remote sensing, scientific programming, and interdisciplinary research. I focus on land-cover and land-use change, conservation biology, urban ecology, fire ecology, and ecosystem services.

I have nine years of professional experience working with geospatial and environmental data. Since publishing my first lead-author study in 2017, I have contributed to scientific publications and developed data products that support research, conservation, and environmental decision-making.
